EKIDIO SOUND Plugins

Fifteen analog-inspired plugins for macOS — eight compressors, a character EQ, a soft tube air enhancer, an analog tape machine, a limiter and three mastering tools. Each has its own character, a faceplate that feels like real gear, and the essential controls right up front. Free.

One family, one way of working

Every unit gets you there in one or two moves. The rarely touched settings live in the fold-out EKI drawer, so the faceplate stays clean.

Character first

Tube, transformer, FET and console colour you can hear — with a clean setting where it makes sense.

AUTO GAIN

Keeps the output at the input level and locks the gain knob at 0 dB. On at startup on the compressors; on the mastering tools it waits in the EKI drawer, off.

Parallel MIX

Phase-aligned dry/wet blend on every unit — no comb filtering, no hollow sound.

Living meters

Glowing VU meters, gain-reduction scales and LEDs that follow the audio.

Presets

Starting points for vocals, drums, bass, buses and mastering — level-matched.

Resizable

Drag the corner from 75 % to 200 %. Each plugin remembers its size.

Compressor Collection

Eight compressors, from the smoothest optical leveller to the fiercest FET smash.

OPTEKI

Optical leveling amplifier

A smooth optical cell with a two-stage, program-dependent release: the longer it works, the slower it lets go. Just GAIN and PEAK REDUCTION, plus Compress/Limit.

Vocals, bass, acoustic instruments.

FETEKI

FET peak limiter

Lightning-fast attack (down to 20 µs), ratio buttons 4 · 8 · 12 · 20 and the aggressive ALL mode for over-compressed, exciting smash.

Drums, room mics, vocals with attitude, bass.

VARIEKI

Variable-mu dynamics

Precision dual peak + RMS detection for invisibly smooth control, with an optional variable-mu tube colour on top. Two meters: gain reduction and output.

Mix bus, mastering, smooth vocal levelling.

BUSEKI

Stereo bus compressor

The console-style VCA glue: stepped ratio, attack and release with a program-dependent AUTO release and a sidechain high-pass so the kick doesn't pump the mix.

Mix bus, drum bus, the "glue".

APEKI

Punch bus compressor

THRUST keeps the low end punching through, KNEE goes from hard to soft, and TYPE switches between tight feed-forward and smooth vintage feedback. The big red OUTPUT says the rest.

Drum bus, mix bus, punchy bass.

DBEKI

RMS compressor / limiter

True RMS detection with program-dependent attack and a constant 120 dB/s release — the classic snap on kick and snare. OverEasy soft knee in one click, BELOW/ABOVE threshold LEDs.

Kick, snare, bass, guitars.

TUBEKI

Opto tube compressor

A creamy optical cell feeding a tube gain stage: drive the GAIN for warmth. Fixed, Manual or combined attack/release, plus parallel compression.

Vocals, bass, acoustic guitar, mix bus warmth.

FAIREKI

Variable-mu limiter

Feedback variable-mu tube limiting that gets steeper the harder you push, six time constants (5 and 6 program-dependent) and LAT/VERT mid-side mode.

Mix bus, mastering, drums and vocals that need weight.

EQ, Color & Tape

Tone shaping, top-end sheen and tape warmth with the same family feel — and the same AUTO GAIN.

DYNEKI-6

Six-band character EQ + dynamics

HPF, four musical bands and a gold AIR band, with two voices: BRIT (smooth and musical) and PUNCH (tight and forward). Switch to M/S for separate Mid and Side EQ, then add warmth with SAT and glue with the one-knob COMP.

Vocals, drums, bass, buses, mastering.

SATEKI

Soft tube air enhancer

Three tube stages that each work only above their own frequency — LOW, MID and HIGH, tunable from 5 to 16 kHz. Quiet high-frequency detail is lifted a lot, loud peaks hardly at all, in the spirit of the classic studio noise-reduction "air trick": silky shine that never gets harsh. MIX for a parallel blend.

Vocals, overheads, acoustic instruments, mix bus.

TAPEKI

Analog tape machine

Three machines — STUDIO, MASTER and CASSETTE — each with its own reels, tape and character. A magnetic hysteresis model gives real tape compression and glue, with head bump, speed-dependent top end (7.5 · 15 · 30 ips), calibration, bias and repro EQ. WOW & FLUTTER pulls the tape (with a little dirt at the top), NOISE brings the hiss — and the reels spin while it plays.

Drums, bass, mix bus, anything that needs warmth and glue.

Mastering

Three new tools for the end of the chain — each with its own installer. AUTO GAIN is there on all three, but off by default, and every knob starts at a neutral 0: with nothing turned, the signal passes through untouched.

EK1

One-knob-per-job mastering chain

A whole mastering chain on one light, brushed faceplate. TONE: AIR (11.6 kHz shelf), FOCUS (a presence bell that widens into the 2–3 kHz range as you push it) and BASS PUNCH (41 Hz shelf) — the same curves as MASTEQUI. LOUDNESS: PUSH adds analog-style density, and the big VOLUME knob drives a two-stage limiter: a slow level stage keeps the body steady while a smooth, fast peak stage catches the hits — so it gets loud and stays tight instead of pumping or softening the transients. SHAPE: TRANSIENT, WIDTH (above 150 Hz only, the low end stays mono) and OUTPUT, with a backlit VU.

The EKI drawer holds the fine tuning: FOCUS FREQ (300 Hz – 5 kHz), TRANSIENT attack and release, the VOLUME release and AUTO GAIN.

Mastering, mix bus, quick loud references.

MASTEQUI

Three-band mastering EQ

Three stepped bells — LOW (11–570 Hz), MID (125 Hz – 6.2 kHz) and HIGH (3.4–25 kHz), each with 24 frequency steps and five Q settings — plus a low shelf (41 Hz, or 83 Hz with LOW SHIFT) and an 11.6 kHz air shelf. Analog-modelled curves, 2× linear-phase oversampling so even the top bells keep their shape up to 20 kHz.

RANGE in the EKI drawer sets the gain knobs to ±6, ±9 or ±12 dB for fine moves or bold ones.

Mastering, mix bus, stems.

Download MASTEQUI

EKIPPER

Soft clipper

One job, done softly: DRIVE pushes the signal into a warm, rounded clip curve that never turns hard, then OUTPUT sets the level. Shave peaks before the limiter, or lean on it for density and glue. 2×, 4× or 8× oversampling in the EKI drawer.

Drums, mix bus, before the limiter.

Limiter

The last step before the bounce — with its own installer, so you can add it next to the collection.

LIMEKI

Density & clean limiter

Two limiters in one rack, wired left to right. DENSITY (burgundy) is the thick, analog-style loudness stage: THRESHOLD pushes the signal into a fast peak catcher with a smooth, musical release (SLOW / FAST, STEREO / DUAL), a soft-clip top and a parallel MIX. CLEAN (blue) is a transparent look-ahead true-peak limiter: PUSH the level, set the CEILING, and an adaptive hold keeps even the low end free of distortion.

Use either half, or both. The AUTO −14 LUFS button in the EKI drawer rides PUSH to streaming loudness, with a short-term LUFS display and a glass LED output meter. No AUTO GAIN here — its whole job is to make things louder.

Mix bus, mastering, drum bus, streaming masters.

Install

  1. Download the DMG and open it. The collection (eleven plugins), LIMEKI, EK1, MASTEQUI and EKIPPER each have their own installer — install whichever you like.
  2. Double-click Install EKIDIO SOUND Plugins.pkg (or the single-plugin one, e.g. Install EKIDIO SOUND EK1.pkg). If macOS says it cannot verify the developer: click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Restart your DAW. In Logic Pro, if a plugin does not show up: Plug-in Manager → Reset & Rescan Selection.
  4. The plugins appear under the manufacturer EKIDIO SOUND.

Uninstall: delete the *EKI.component files (and LIMEKI, EK1, MASTEQUI, EKIPPER.component) from /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components.
