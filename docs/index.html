AUTO GAIN

Keeps the output at the input level and locks the gain knob at 0 dB. On at startup on the compressors, DYNEKI-6 and SATEKI; off on TAPEKI, MASTEQUI, REVEKI and DELEKI. EK1 and LIMEKI set their level with AUTO −14 LUFS instead.

Parallel MIX

Phase-aligned dry/wet blend on every unit — no comb filtering, no hollow sound.

Living meters

EKIDIO RACK

Effect rack for EKIDIO SOUND plugins

Your whole chain in one window. Six slots, each one takes any EKIDIO SOUND effect installed on your Mac: pick it from the slot's menu and its own, original panel opens right below. Drag a slot onto another to change the order, step through them with the arrow keys, and save the complete chain – every effect, its place and all its settings – as a single rack preset. Press TAB to turn the rack around and look at the cables.

51 ready-made chains in six groups – Mastering, Drums, Vocal, Bass, Guitar and Synth – built from the EKIDIO SOUND effects and level-matched, so you hear the tone, not just more volume. Your own presets can be saved, loaded and deleted.

Vocal and instrument chains, buses, mastering.

Needs at least one EKIDIO SOUND effect installed — the rack hosts the effects you already have.

OPTEKI

Optical leveling amplifier

The master of invisible smoothness. Its silky optical cell evens out the dynamics while the music's natural breath stays untouched. The deeper it digs into compression, the more gently it lets go – on vocals and acoustic instruments it brings that instant velvet that makes a track sound like a finished song. Thinking of the master bus? Try it!

Vocals, bass, acoustic instruments.

FETEKI

FET peak limiter

Lightning-fast energy and raw power. No politeness here: it catches even the wildest transients like a lightning strike, and the iconic ALL mode gives drums and room tracks a biting, exciting crunch and analog attitude that almost blows the speakers apart.

Drums, room mics, vocals with attitude, bass.

VARIEKI

Variable-mu dynamics

The refined luxury of the studio's golden age. Its dual detection wraps around stray peaks almost unnoticed, while the vari-mu tube action quietly makes the whole picture sound “master-done”.

Mix bus, mastering, smooth vocal levelling.

BUSEKI

Stereo bus compressor

The legendary console glue that forges your tracks into one unified, pulsing whole. It pulls the mix together without the kick swallowing the space, and gives it that tight, professional posture that turns a song into a finished radio hit.

Mix bus, drum bus, the "glue".

APEKI

Punch bus compressor

For when you want the drums to hit you in the chest. Thanks to the THRUST circuit, the lows of the kick and bass break through the mix untouched and with elemental force, while the tracks get an instant, explosive, tight American character.

DBEKI

RMS compressor / limiter

It responds to energy the way the human ear hears it: kick and snare get exactly the slap they need to cut through the densest arrangement, while OverEasy mode keeps the limits soft. You won't hear this anywhere else!

Kick, snare, bass, guitars.

TUBEKI

Opto tube compressor

It blends the finesse of an optical cell with the glowing soul of overdriven vacuum tubes. Push the signal into it and it doesn't just compress – it brings thin drums, bass or vocals to life.

Vocals, bass, acoustic guitar, mix bus warmth.

FAIREKI

Variable-mu limiter

The uncrowned king of vintage iron. The harder you drive it, the more majestically and massively it holds the picture together. In LAT/VERT mode the surround of the stereo image comes alive.

DELEKI

Clean stereo delay

Crystal-clear digital echoes that bounce from left to right. TIME in milliseconds or in note values locked to your DAW (straight, dotted, triplet), PING-PONG or STEREO with an OFFSET for width, LOW CUT and HIGH CUT inside the feedback loop so every repeat gets cleaner and darker, and DUCK that tucks the echoes under the vocal and lets them bloom in the gaps. It starts on a dub-style dotted-eighth bounce you will love on the first play.

Vocal throws, guitars, synths, dub, wide doubling.

Install

  1. Download the DMG and open it. The eight compressors come in one installer (Compressor Collection); every other plugin has its own — install whichever you like.
  2. Double-click Install EKIDIO SOUND Compressor Collection.pkg (or the single-plugin one, e.g. Install EKIDIO SOUND EK1.pkg). If macOS says it cannot verify the developer: click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Restart your DAW. In Logic Pro, if a plugin does not show up: Plug-in Manager → Reset & Rescan Selection.
  4. The plugins appear under the manufacturer EKIDIO SOUND.

Uninstall: delete the *EKI.component files (and DYNEKI, SATEKI, TAPEKI, LIMEKI, EK1, MASTEQUI, PULTEKI, EKIPPER.component) from /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components.
